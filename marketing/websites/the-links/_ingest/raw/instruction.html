Instruction

We have partnered with several experienced instructors to help hone your skills!

  • Adult Instruction

  • Beginners and kids

  • Experienced Golfers who want to up their game

  • 1:1 and Group rates available

Please contact one of the below directly to setup a time or learn more! If you have general questions, please CONTACT US and we will point you in the right direction.

Barry Wallin

PGA GOLF PROFESSIONAL

Barry Wallin is a Class A PGA Golf Professional, and Director of Golf Instruction at Emerald Greens Golf Course in Hastings, MN. There he oversees a staff of 30, and a program that serves over 900 students in the youth and adult golf instructional programs.

He has coached the Rosemount girls golf teams since 1991. He is a 3-time Section 3AAA Girls Golf Coach of the Year (2009, 2014, 2017), and a 2-time MN State AAA Girls Golf Coach of the Year (2009, 2014).

He was inducted into the MN State Golf Coaches Association Hall of Fame in 2018.

Barry’s philosophy: I believe in helping each person build on the good things that they are already doing in their swing, in order to help them deepen their enjoyment of the game of golf.

barry.wallin@futuresclub.org - 651-245-5899

David Anderson

David is an Assistant Golf Professional at the Meadows at Mystic Lake. Located in Prior Lake, MN, he has been on staff since 2022.

Prior to entering the golf industry full-time, his experience includes playing collegiate golf at the University of St. Thomas (St. Paul, MN), Head Boys Varsity Golf Coach for Eden Prairie High School (2020-2022), former director of the Get a Grip Youth Golf League, and spending summers as a caddie for various different private clubs around the Twin Cities.

Philosophy: Golf has a different meaning for everyone. I believe it is one of the best activities one can do for their mental and physical health. My teaching is centered around being an active listener, goal setting, and crafting a personalized plan for each and every student. This is a game of a lifetime, so why not learn the tools to get the most enjoyment out of your game.

David.Anderson@mysticlakegolf.com - 952.233.2890

Tamara Colucci

Tamara has been coaching golf for 25 years. Starting golf at the age of six, she played high school and college golf and continues to coach high school golfers. She has coached at Watertown High School, Apple Valley High School, and is currently the varsity head golf coach at Eastview High School. She has brought several players to the MSHSL State Tournament, and most recently has helped her team place 5th and 3rd at the State Tournament. Tamara is also a golf instructor with Barry Wallin, PGA Golf Professional, at the Emerald Greens Golf Course Camps. Tamara is willing to help anyone wanting to learn how to play golf or make improvements to their game.

tcgolfer44@gmail.com - 612-306-4409

Nick Stoulil

Nick’s passion is to spread the knowledge he has learned to others and hopefully give them the tools to truly understand this great game we all love and hate! Nick’s experience includes: Played in over 30 Mini Tour events, Golf Academy of America graduate, Made Academic Directors List, Teaching Certificate, Completed Players Ability Test at Ken McDonald GC in Tempe, AZ, Earned Players Credentials, Tournament Operations experience.
