Candidate note — home-soft

The one committed idea: the page is lit, not tinted. Warmth arrives as a light source with a direction — a pendant pool on every dark band, a warm-tinted (never grey) shadow under every raised surface, a warm paper ground with no pure white anywhere — so the ink can stay at full strength. And the action colour is the light: ember on a lit field, amber on a dark one. That is why the hero CTA is not a white rectangle.

Preview limitations, stated

  • Type is an approximation. The proposal in token-sets/soft.json is Fraunces (variable, opsz + SOFT axes, 400/600/700) for display, replacing Bitter, with Open Sans retained for body and promoted to 17px / 1.65. This review surface loads no network resources, so display renders in the system serif and body in the system humanist sans. Judge the overlay’s declaration, not this rendering.
  • Nothing here is a photograph. Three slots are drawn as inline SVG at the shoot’s own 3:2 ratio, in the measured register (warm near-black room, warm pendant, one cool lit screen). No stock imagery, no AI imagery, no Lakeville frame standing in for Stillwater.
  • The hero slot is desktop-only. Below 1024px the hero photograph becomes the section’s own light rather than a separate frame, so the trust strip stays reachable within roughly one hero screen-height on a 390×844 phone.

One string added beyond the §7 copy sheet — flag it or keep it

  • $35 / hour · per bay, not per person · Summer 2026 rate, published through 3 Oct 2026 on the Play card. Every value is verbatim from the spec’s §8 data sheet, and it is what gives Play visual seniority over Improve. It is one block to delete if the copy sheet is treated as closed.
