## The email

**Subject:** The Links website — a short list of confirmations (and one date we need to beat)

Hi [name],

We've rebuilt the site for both venues and it's ready for you to look at. Before we put it
live there's a short list of things only you can answer.

**The one with a deadline:** the current site's only published rate — $35/hour — is about to run
out. After that date the site has no price on it at all. Winter is your busiest season, so that's
the gap worth closing first.

Most of what follows is us checking what we already pulled from your current site. If a line
is right, just say "yes". If it's wrong, correct it.

### 1. Hours — please confirm, and tell us about winter

Your `/rates` page states both schedules and we've carried them across exactly as published:

- **Lakeville:** 11am–9pm, seven days
- **Stillwater:** Sun 12–7, Mon 12–9, Tue–Fri 3–9, Sat 10–10

**Are those still right?** And what changes for winter — the published schedule runs out at the end
of September.

One more from the same page: you list **Memorial Day, Independence Day and Labor Day as closed**.
Labor Day is 7 September, which falls inside the hours we're publishing. Are there other closures we
should carry, so nobody drives to a locked door?

### 2. Rates — the end date, then winter

**Your own site gives two end dates for the $35 rate.** Your homepage says "valid until September
30th, 2026"; your `/rates` page says "through October 3, 2026". We publish **30 September**, on the
grounds that advertising a rate that has already ended is the more expensive mistake — but three
days is three days, and it's a price. Which is right?

Then the real question: what's the **winter** rate, and does it vary by peak/off-peak or by day?

### 3. Leagues

This is the big one. Your leagues page currently says "check back", and we're heading into
league season. For each venue: **which nights, what format, team size, how many weeks, what it
costs, and when registration opens.**

We've also built something no competitor site we surveyed has — a waitlist that works even when
registration is closed, so you stop losing people who arrive at the wrong moment.

### 4. Groups and events

No pricing is published today. What's a party or corporate outing actually cost — package
price or minimum spend, how many people, what's included, how much notice you need? Does
Stillwater take events too?

### 5. Lessons

Four coaches are listed with no prices. What does a lesson cost, and does the fee include the
bay time?

### 6. Cancellation policy

You publish this in three places — "free cancellation at least 48 hours before your reservation,
inside 48 hours you may be charged for the full bay" — and we've carried it across, because it's
the single most-asked question before someone books and almost no venue answers it. **Is it still
current?**

Three things nobody publishes and we haven't guessed: whether a booking can be **moved** rather than
cancelled and by when, what happens to a **no-show**, and what happens if **you** close and someone
had a booking.

### 7. Phone numbers — one of these is on the Stillwater page right now

Three numbers appear across your current site: **612-699-0526**, **612-699-0527**, and
**612-619-1747**. Which belongs to which venue, and is the third one still live?

**The one we need first: what number rings the Stillwater desk?** Your footer says
**612-699-0527**. Your own `/stillwater` page says **612-699-0526** — and so does the Greater
Stillwater Chamber listing. But 612-699-0526 is also Lakeville's number, so our guess is that those
two are showing your main line and the footer is the one drawing the distinction. **We've gone with
612-699-0527 and flagged it on the site as unconfirmed** rather than quietly switching, because a
wrong phone number is worse than a marked one. One word from you and the flag comes off.

### 8. Simulators

Some pages say **GolfZon NX**, others say **TwoVision**. Which is in which venue? Worth fixing
quickly — AI assistants are currently telling people you run TwoVision, because they're reading your
older pages.

Two more, while you're asking whoever installed them:

- **The exact model and the plate in each bay.** GolfZon's tour requires "either a TwoVision or
  TwoVisionNX and a Dual Plate or Motion Plate". If that's what you have, you can say your bays are
  tour-spec — which is a genuinely strong line and nobody local is using it. We won't write it until
  you confirm both halves.
- **Do you want TrackMan and Full Swing named on your own site?** Our Simulators page currently
  mentions them as the other systems people see around the Twin Cities. It's useful for search and
  it's honest, but it's your site and your call.

### 9. When Stillwater actually opened

Your homepage says **Thursday 29 January**. Your `/our-story` page says **February**. We've written
"early 2026" everywhere, which is true either way, but we'd rather print the real date — and so
would the people searching for it.

### 10. Your printed menu and your website disagree about your opening hours

Not a website problem — a printing one, and worth knowing before you reprint. The menu graphic says
**"HOURS: SUN-SAT 8AM-10PM"**. Your `/rates` page says **11am–9pm daily** at Lakeville and a
different schedule again at Stillwater. We've published the `/rates` version, which we think is the
right one — but the menu is the one document a customer physically holds while they're sitting in
your building.

### 11. Two things we took off the site rather than guess

- **The map pin.** We'd been publishing latitude and longitude for both venues in the code Google
  reads. They were our own approximations, and when we checked, Lakeville's was about two miles from
  the building. Both are now removed — Google works fine off the street address. If you'd like a
  precise pin back, send the one from your Google Business Profile.
- **Gift cards: do they cover food and drink, or only bay time?** The page said they covered both.
  Nothing you publish says either way, and it's the first thing the person receiving one will try to
  do with it. Also: does the card arrive by email, how fast, and can it be used the same day? That
  last one is the whole question for a 23 December buyer.

### 12. Photography

Your Lakeville photos are genuinely good and we're using them. **There are no photographs of
Stillwater at all** — one phone snap taken before opening. A half-day shoot there would make
the biggest single difference to how the site feels. Can we get that booked?

### 13. Google — the one that isn't about the website

**We can't find a Google Business Profile for Stillwater.** Your own site's map link points at
the bowling alley. That's six months of local searches going nowhere, and it matters more than
anything on this list. Does one exist? If not, we should create and verify it this week.

### 14. Reviews

We won't publish a quote we can't attribute. Are there reviews or customers you'd be happy for
us to quote by name, and can you send the Google review links for both venues?

### 15. Memberships — three things to flag

- **LinksFlex "Anytime" costs more than turning up.** It works out to **roughly $40–46 an hour**,
  against a $35 walk-up rate. We've held it back rather than publish it — worth a look before it
  goes live.
- **Can an Individual member bring guests into their bay at no charge — yes or no?** Your price card
  says "Only available for the package purchaser" and "No Guest Fees" in the same breath, and the
  membership terms say a package is "only for the purchaser". We've told visitors we're confirming it
  rather than picking an answer for you, because this is the one that costs somebody money at the
  till.
- **What do extra hours cost** once a member has used their two a day? Your bullet says "purchase
  more hours anytime" and stops there.

### Also useful, whenever you get to it

- **Three words** for how you want the site to sound. Ours currently reads confident, warm,
  not stuffy — deliberately written so someone who has never held a club doesn't feel out of
  place. Tell us if that's wrong.
- **Anything you must never say** — claims, competitors, anything legal.
- **Logo files** if you have them. There's no logo artwork anywhere at the moment, and the
  favicon still shows the old Lakeville Links badge, so every link you share previews the old
  brand.
- **Junior programs** — what's running in 2027?
- **The menu** as text. It only exists as pictures today, which means Google can't read it, AI
  assistants can't quote it, and neither can a screen reader.
- **Who'll keep the site updated** after launch? We'll show them — hours, prices and league
  status are all editable without a developer.

Thanks — the first eight get us to a site that can go live. The rest can follow.

[sign-off]
