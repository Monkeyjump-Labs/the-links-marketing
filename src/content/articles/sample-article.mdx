## Articles vs. blog posts

Articles use the same schema as blog posts but render under `/article/`. The
distinction is purely editorial — keep both, or delete the collection you do not
need.

This file is at `src/content/articles/sample-article.mdx` and renders at
`/article/sample-article/`.
